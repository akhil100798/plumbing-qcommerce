All evidence-invalid files and customer-interactions scripts/results/logs are INVALID MOCKED EVIDENCE, including copies outside evidence-invalid. Screenshots sharing hashes with that directory are also invalid. Historical screenshots without proven real runtime provenance are excluded from current certification.

admin-build.log is STALE PRE-SYNC EVIDENCE. admin-build-postsync.log supersedes it. Earlier backend startup failures, first seeding-race 401 responses, and interrupted transport probes remain preserved; recovered results are separate.

The first public harness attempted to click a correctly disabled short-phone button; that was a harness error. Its script/log are preserved. The first Plumber registration scan actually captured the login screen; use public-focused-recovery.json for registration. Never treat the mislabeled first scan as registration evidence.

Product-provided static fallback catalog was observed during a transport failure. It is a product behavior finding, not real inventory proof and not an agent-generated mock.

Token/password-bearing environment files and historical raw process/database logs are excluded from the certification manifest. Canonical current outputs contain no credential values.
